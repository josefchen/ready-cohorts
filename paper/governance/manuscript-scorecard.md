## Decision

The current manuscript is scientifically suitable as a scoped arXiv artifact
preprint after the release checklist is closed. It is not yet the flagship
top-tier systems paper. That claim depends on a finite online route compactor
that converts `P* - F` into achieved share `A` while improving CPU use or raw
P99 without harming exactness, task utility, or inference SLOs.
